How to play ▾

Both phones on the same Wi-Fi. One player taps Host and reads out the 4-letter code; the other types it in and taps Join.

Tap to rotate · drag sideways to move · drag down to soft drop · flick down to slam · flick up to hold.

Clearing 2, 3 or 4 lines at once sends 1, 2 or 4 grey rows to your rival (more for combos and back-to-back fours). Clearing lines also cancels rows heading your way — watch the red bar. Last board standing wins the round; first to 2 rounds wins.
